Welcome to the game The killer!

Control keys:

Escape. Exit the game.
F1. View who is online and how many kills someone has.
F2. Check the server uptime.
F3. Check the ping.
F4. Check the message of the day.
F5. View the server stats.
F6. Check how many packets were sent and received.
F7. Enable and disable online and offline messages.
F8. Enable or disable chat messages.
F9. Enable or disable the speaking of all game messages.
F10. Select language channel.
F11. Enable or disable death messages.
Grave. Open the tracking zones menu.
1. Select the weapon fists.
2. Select the weapon feet.
From 3 to = select other weapons, if they are installed in in slots
Tab. Move forward through the inventory.
Shift+Tab. Move back through the inventory.
Q. Turn Left.
W. Track the player.
E. Turn right.
Shift+e. Join the event
Alt+e. Leave the event
R. Reload your weapon.
T. Open the player tracking menu.
Shift+t. Copy Player Menu.
Shift+y. Write to Admin_tell.
U. Write a message to the team.
Shift+U. Open the team menu.
I. Open your inventory.
In the inventory.
Shift+space. Auction the item.
Alt+space. Give the item to a nearby player.

O. Bid on the auction.
P. Check the players nearby.
A. View ammo.
S. Stand up or sit down.
F. Check your direction.
G. Camera.
Hold down the letter G-Press the arrows or pages up or down to move camera.
Hold down g and press t to check at what coordinates and on what tile the camera is located.

Shift+G. When the game guess the number is started enter your value.
H. Check your health and shields.
K. Cancel your bid at the auction.
Shift+K. Cancel the auction if it is yours.
l. Check the level, experience information.
Shift+L. View how much experience is left to gain until the next level.
Z. Check the location.
X. If you were stopped by Stun_grenade or the Stun tile, unstun.
C. Check the coordinates.
V. Open the social menu.
Shift+V. Reply to the last private message.
B. Open the build menu, " Attention-Only works if you have the rank of Administrator or builder.
n. Track the zone.
m. Look around for lying objects.
Comma. Go back through the messages.
Dot. Go forward through the messages.
Left bracket. Go back through the buffers.
Right bracket. Go forward through the buffers.
/. Open the chat.
Left or right control. Shooting.
Space. Jump.
Up, right, down, and left arrows. Walking.
Pages up or down. Go up or down stairs.
Alt+pages up or down. Turn the radio volume up or down
Home or End. Turn up or down the volume of the game
Enter. Transition between locations.
Shift+Enter. Use the item.
Shift + numbers from 1 to 0. Assign items to the appropriate slot.
Alt+ numbers from 1 to 0. Use items in slots.
Backslash. Open a local chat.

Have a nice game!
